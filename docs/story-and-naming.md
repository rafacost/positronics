# Positronics: story and naming

Draft 0.1, 2026-11-19. Inspired by Stephen King's *Dark Tower* universe (North Central Positronics). **We only borrow names and the premise. We never quote or paste text from the books, and never use logos or art.** Fan-flavour naming is common in modpacks, but before publishing, review the final name list once (especially the pack name and anything trademark-like).

## The premise (our own words)

The world has moved on. What's left is an island hanging over the void. Long ago the Old People stopped trusting old powers and rebuilt the foundations of the world with **machines**: the Beams that hold reality together, the Doors between worlds, and the great corporation that built them all, **North Central Positronics**.

You wake up on a fragment of that world with a single job: **rebuild the Positronics network**, tier by tier. Restore power, restore the Beams, and at the end open a **Door** to other worlds.

Tone: hopeful reconstruction, not horror. The darker parts of the source (sorcery, the Crimson King, the Breakers, todash) **stay out**, in line with the pack's no-magic rule.

### Notes on fit
- **The Calla** for LV makes the bees, Pam's farming and Spice of Life chapter feel natural: a farming town with a first factory.
- **Lud** for MV: "the city of machines in ruins" fits the step where automation becomes mandatory (AE2 autocrafting, the Seed II Core).
- **The Doors** are why Stargate Journey belongs in the story, not just as a bolted-on mod.
- **Via Aurea**: there's no need to force it into Dark Tower names. Its devotional craft stays its own voice: the player's faith, inside a rebuilt world.

## Lore delivery (low effort, high flavour)
- Short **quest descriptions** in our own words, as found NCP memos, maintenance logs and corporate notices, with dry humour ("Clearance Level 2 required. Please do not lick the conduit.").
- **Item tooltips** on our own items (Positronic Cores, Clearance Cards).
- An optional **Patchouli "NCP Field Manual"**, later, which explains the mechanics in-world.
