## The world has moved on.

You wake on a small island of grey stone in an empty sky. There are no ores to mine, no mobs to fight, no villagers
to trade with, and no Nether or End to escape to. North Central Positronics left you a field kit, a set of memos and
one job: **rebuild the network, tier by tier, from ULV to UHV.**

**Positronics** is a tech-only **GregTech + ProjectE skyblock** for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge). Everything you make
comes from rock, water, sunlight and atoms, and every tier has to be earned.

## What makes it different

- **Strata, not ores.** Resources are hammered out of gravel, sifted, then produced by Ore Generators. Later the
  Reclamation Sifter Array turns that into a production line.
- **Atoms are the currency.** Positronic Memory (ProjectE) learns **elements only**, never ingots. The Atomic
  Reclaimer breaks biomass and rock into atoms, and their EMC fills Klein Stars. Full stars power your generators
  and build the Positronic Cores that open each tier.
- **Power you have to plan.** Solar panels feed GregTech through energy converters, and the sun sets every evening.
  Fuel burns too, but every bucket is EMC you didn't spend on iron.
- **Survival that doesn't stop.** Thirst, body temperature, four seasons, and a steady hunger burn, AFK included.
  Eat a variety and you're rewarded for it.
- **A story in memos.** Ten chapters, each one a set of North Central Positronics field memos, with loading-screen tips
  numbered after the memo they belong to.
- **Side paths that matter:** bees (Productive Bees), real chemistry (Alchemistry), simulated mobs (Hostile Neural
  Networks), rockets and a Stargate, storage from drawers to AE2, and a company shop paid in NCP Scrip.
- **No magic.** Not a little, not as a shortcut. If it isn't science, it isn't here.

## Before you start

- **Beta.** The whole pack is playable, ULV to UHV. It is a work in progress: so far it has been played through to
  the end of River Crossing (ULV), so expect rough edges past that, and please report them.
- **Single player.** Multiplayer hasn't been tested.
- **Memory:** give Minecraft at least **6 GB**.
- **Start a new world.** The first quests teach you to drink, eat and hammer. Read them; the island is not forgiving.
- **It's GregTech.** Expect long chains, real multiblocks and a slow, steady climb. That's the point.

## Found a problem?

That's what the beta is for. Report it on **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/rafacost/positronics/issues/new/choose)**
(issues only, no Discord yet) and include:

- the pack version (shown on the main menu),
- what you did, and what happened instead,
- `logs/latest.log` and `logs/kubejs/server.log` from your game folder, or the crash report.

The in-game Field Manual has the same instructions (Section 14, Field Reports).
